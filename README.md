# JVEngine Template Library

**The Jazari–Vinci Prototyping Engine** is a product concept for a privacy-first engineering companion. This repository provides platform-neutral Markdown starting points for organizing invention projects and documenting design work. They can be used independently of any JVEngine implementation.

JVEngine is still a concept: its platform, technical architecture, supported providers, monetization, and implementation milestones have not been finalized. These templates are proposals to help explore the project workflow, not a description of shipped product features.

## Templates

- [Invention project](templates/invention-project.md): Capture a concept, the problem it addresses, constraints, and next steps.
- [Design iteration](templates/design-iteration.md): Record changes to a design and the reasoning and observations behind them.
- [Experiment log](templates/experiment-log.md): Plan and document a prototype test.
- [Patent-preparation notes](templates/patent-preparation.md): Organize invention details and questions for a qualified patent professional.

## Use

Copy the relevant Markdown file into your project workspace and fill it in. Keep one design-iteration or experiment-log file per iteration or test, and link related notes from your invention project. Adapt or remove sections that do not fit your work.

These are plain-text files and make no assumptions about an app, platform, storage system, or AI provider. Choose where to store them. If using an AI service, do not share project details unless you have chosen that provider and understand what information will be sent. Never put API keys, passwords, or other credentials in project notes.

## Privacy and legal notes

The templates encourage keeping project notes under your control; they do not themselves provide storage, encryption, or privacy guarantees. Patent-preparation notes are for organizing information and are not legal advice, a patent application, or a guarantee of patentability. Consult a qualified patent professional for legal advice and filing decisions.
